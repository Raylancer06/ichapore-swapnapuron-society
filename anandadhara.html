Bridging the Gap Between Food Waste and Starvation

Anandadhara is our dedicated project serving the homeless and helpless people across North 24 Parganas.

In our localities like Ichapore, Barrackpore, Palta, and Shyamnagar, there are numerous wedding halls and festival houses where large amounts of wholesome, delicious food are often left unused due to guest fluctuation.

Our volunteers step in immediately: we collect those surplus food items, inspect quality, foil-pack them separately and hygienically, and distribute them to vagrants, lonely elders, and destitute people living on remote pavements and railway platforms.

Additionally, when orders are canceled in local food outlets or when kind citizens celebrate birthdays and anniversaries by sponsoring meals, Anandadhara ensures that warmth and nutritious food reach the hungriest bellies.

Night Food Rescue Patrol

Rapid foil packaging and midnight distribution to destitute families at Ichapore, Palta, Barrackpore, and Shyamnagar stations.

How It Works

Our 4-Step Food Recovery Pipeline

Ensuring zero food waste and 100% food safety and dignity for recipients.

notifications_active

1. Alert & Pickup

Wedding halls, banquet caterers, and citizens call our volunteer hotline when clean extra food is available.

check_circle

2. Quality Check

Volunteers perform sensory and temperature checks ensuring food is fresh, hot, and hygienic before packaging.

inventory_2

3. Foil Packaging

Rice, curries, and breads are neatly sealed in individual food-grade aluminum foil containers with spoon and water.

sports_motorsports

4. Night Delivery

Our mobile volunteers deliver directly to people sleeping on stations, temple steps, and under bridges.
