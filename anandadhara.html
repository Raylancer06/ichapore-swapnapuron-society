ANANDADHARA

Your help is very important to provide these people with a little better food

Anandadhara collects surplus wedding banquet food and canceled food orders, packaging them into fresh hygienic foil meals distributed nightly to homeless and vagrant people at local stations.

rice_bowl Why Anandadhara

Feeding the Helpless with Dignity and Compassion

Anandadhara project is dedicated to the homeless and helpless people. We have many festival houses in Ichhapur, where many times food is wasted due to insufficient number of guests on many occasions.

Our pleasure is to collect those leftover food items, foil them separately, and distribute them to the vagrants and helpless people living in the remote areas and stations of Ichhapur, Barakpur, Palta, and Shyamnagar. Even in many food shops of Barakpur, if any ordered food is canceled, we collect and distribute it so no edible food is ever dumped.
