local_library Why Vidharthi

Empowering Street Children with the Tools to Learn

Our project, Vidharthi, is a dedicated initiative aimed at supporting the education of underprivileged street children who often face significant barriers to learning.

Recognizing that these children are the future of our society, we believe that equitable access to educational resources is essential. To this end, we are committed to distributing essential school supplies completely free of charge, including School Bags, Notebooks, Pens, Pencil Boxes, and other related materials. This initiative not only empowers these children with the tools they need for basic education but also serves as a lifeline for their families, helping to break the cycle of poverty.
