## Brand & Style

This design system channels an urgent, high-conviction humanitarian voice. Melding brutalist clarity with high-end editorial refinement, it avoids generic SaaS aesthetics in favor of purposeful, tactile structure, crisp line work, and dramatic typographic hierarchy. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Editorial Brutalism**: Strict rectilinear alignments, structural 1px dividers, tabular numerals, and condensed headline scales that command respect and convey organizational rigor.
- **Warm Tactile Grounding**: High-contrast, deeply legible text set against warm linen substrates instead of clinical, sterile grays.
- **Intentional Restraint**: Deliberate avoidance of soft gradient cards, decorative colored blurs, or gratuitous iconography. Visual gravity is established through uncompromising type contrast, stark geometric borders, and measured splashes of urgent terracotta crimson.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate human and civic gravity. Rather than high-tech neons or digital blues, the scheme relies on earth-derived pigments rooted in urgency, justice, and grounded endurance.

- **Primary (`#C23A22`)**: A saturated terracotta crimson reserved for urgent actions, critical campaign counters, accent borders, and primary status indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0C1A1A`)**: A near-black deep forest ink. Serves as the primary typographic anchor, providing profound contrast without the harshness of raw pitch black.
- **Tertiary (`#2F5022`)**: Deep arboreal olive. Used for environmental initiatives, secondary impact indicators, and sustainable advocacy markers.
- **Neutral & Surfaces (`#FAF8F5`, `#F3EFE9`)**: Linen and parchment bases. They replace standard white/gray palettes to lend a humanistic, publication-grade feel to interfaces.
- **Structural Dividers (`#E7E2D9`)**: Precise hairpins used for delineating tabular content, cards, and navigation bands.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an intentional friction between bold civic agitation and thoughtful literary testimony:

- **Headlines & Metrics (`Oswald`)**: A condensed, commanding grotesque typeface applied in tight tracking and prominent scale. Used for display statistics, impact counters, and section headers to evoke journalistic urgency and field-poster authority.
- **Narrative & Editorial (`Newsreader`)**: A classic, legible serif providing warmth, intellectual credibility, and literary grace for human stories, editorial dispatches, mission statements, and long-form reporting.
- **Structural UI & Meta (`Space Grotesk`)**: A precise, geometric mono-hybrid utilized for operational labels, tabular tickers, data captions, micro-badges, and interactive inputs. Monospaced in feel to ground the interfaces in raw, non-profit transparency.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an architectonic 12-column grid bound together with persistent 1px hairline rules.

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - Desktop: 12-column fluid grid, 48px canvas margins, 24px internal gutters. Max container width: 1360px.
  - Tablet: 8-column layout, 32px canvas margins, 20px gutters.
  - Mobile: 4-column layout, 20px canvas margins, 16px gutters.
- **Hairline Framing**: Modules, cards, and headers do not float over space; they lock into an edge-to-edge matrix separated by single `sand-border` (`#E7E2D9`) grid lines.
- **Section Breaks**: Vertical rhythms leverage larger multipliers (e.g., `space-xl` scaled to 4rem or 6rem) to create ample breathing room between high-density data matrices and editorial copy blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews standard drop shadows, diffused colored glows, and floating layers in favor of editorial, architectural planes:

- **Zero-Shadow Philosophy**: Visual depth is formed through structural surface shifts (`#FAF8F5` base against `#F3EFE9` inset panels) and crisp 1px `#E7E2D9` framing borders.
- **Direct Contrast Overlays**: When a modal or drawer requires prominence, it employs a hard 1px outline paired with a warm translucent backdrop (`rgba(12, 26, 26, 0.45)`) that dims background content with zero blur.
- **Brutalist Layering**: Floating action cues, contextual popovers, or floating banners use crisp, solid 2px offset borders or inverse fills (`#0C1A1A` on `#FAF8F5`) instead of multi-radius elevation curves.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an uncompromising `roundedness: 0` (Sharp) philosophy:
- **Geometry**: All buttons, badges, card containers, inputs, and modal dialogs use strict 90-degree corners.
- **Rhythm**: Sharp angles emphasize the editorial structure, journalistic precision, and unapologetic institutional purpose of the platform.
- **Micro-accents**: Occasional pill tags are strictly avoided; category indicators and taxonomy tags are designed as rectilinear stamped tickets or rectangular micro-table cells.

## Components

### Navigation Bar
- **Form Factor**: Fixed or sticky top bar bound on the bottom by a 1px `#E7E2D9` border.
- **Background**: `#FAF8F5` (100% opaque, no blur).
- **Structure**: Brand logotype flush-left; monospace editorial links centered (`Space Grotesk`, uppercase, tracked +0.08em); single high-contrast direct action button ("Take Action" / "Donate") flush-right in solid `#C23A22` with sharp corners.

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary**: Sharp rectangle, background `#C23A22`, text `#FAF8F5`, uppercase `Space Grotesk` with `0.05em` letter spacing. On hover: shifts to `#9C2612`.
- **Secondary**: Sharp rectangle, background transparent, 1px border `#0C1A1A`, text `#0C1A1A`. On hover: solid `#0C1A1A` fill with `#FAF8F5` text.
- **Editorial Text Action**: Underlined serif link (`Newsreader` italic) accented with a terracotta right-arrow glyph (`→`).

### Metric & Impact Counters
- **Display**: Massive condensed numerals (`Oswald`, bold, 56px–84px) paired with an uppercase monospaced label (`Space Grotesk`) directly below.
- **Container**: Contained in border-delimited grid tiles (`#F3EFE9`), separated by 1px rules rather than individual floating boxes.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Style**: Monospaced stamped badges (`label-sm`).
- **Treatment**: Rectangular, 1px border.
  - Active/Urgent: `#FBEAE7` fill, `#C23A22` text, `#C23A22` border.
  - Neutral/Filed: `#F3EFE9` fill, `#111827` text, `#D3CBC0` border.
  - Environmental/Reform: `#FAF8F5` fill, `#2F5022` text, `#2F5022` border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Flat `#FAF8F5` or `#F3EFE9` background with a 1px solid bottom or full perimeter border in `#E7E2D9`. Focus state switches the border to a 1.5px `#0C1A1A` or `#C23A22`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 100% square. Checkbox indicates selection with a solid `#C23A22` fill and white checkmark; radio uses a sharp inset black square.

### Editorial & Impact Cards
- **Construction**: Never use box shadows or rounded corners. A card is a structural cell created by a 1px `#E7E2D9` border over a `#F3EFE9` surface.
- **Content Hierarchy**: Top metadata tag in `Space Grotesk`, followed by a high-contrast headline in `Oswald`, brief journalistic body excerpt in `Newsreader`, and an inline baseline action footer.
