Who We Are

Ichapore Swapnapuron Society is a registered non-profit organization working since 2009 for the holistic development of society with a focus on Health, Education, Women Empowerment, Environment Protection, and Animal Welfare.

fingerprint Our Identity

Born from a Compassionate Community Dream

Ichapore Swapnapuron Society (ISS) is a registered voluntary organization founded in 2009 by a group of compassionate individuals from Ichapore and nearby areas who came together with a shared dream – to create a better, healthier, and more equitable society.

Over the years, we have grown into a community-driven organization with the active support of volunteers, well-wishers, and partners who believe in our mission. From educating children of daily-wage earners to organizing mobile health clinics, distributing reflective collars for street dogs, and planting saplings for newborns, our grassroots footprint touches lives every single day.

What We Do

Six Pillars of Holistic Grassroots Action

We work at the grassroots level to bring positive and sustainable change in the communities we serve.

health_and_safety

Health

Improving health and well-being through mobile clinics, diagnostic checkups, medicine distribution, and maternal screenings.

school

Education

Promoting quality remedial coaching, English phonics, school bags, and daily nutritious meals for street children.

woman

Women Empowerment

Empowering women through skill development, menstrual hygiene awareness, sanitary pad support, and maternal care.

forest

Environment Protection

Conserving nature and promoting a greener future through tree saplings for newborns (Janmataru) and plastic cleanups.

pets

Animal Welfare

Working for the care and safety of street animals through high-visibility reflective collars (Ayushman Bhava) and community feeding.

groups

Community Development

Building stronger communities across 110+ slums through emergency winter relief, clean water, and disaster aid.
