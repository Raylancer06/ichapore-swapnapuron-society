Purpose & Direction

Our Vision & Mission

visibility

Vision

At Ichapore Swapnapuron Society, our vision is to cultivate a compassionate and equitable society where every child has access to quality education, every woman enjoys optimal health, and every living being is treated with kindness and respect.

Explore Full Mission & Vision arrow_forward
flag

Mission

Our mission is to uplift and empower marginalized individuals and communities through targeted initiatives that focus on Education for Street Children: providing street children with access to quality education, mentorship, and resources that enable them to break the cycle of poverty and realize their full potential.

How You Can Help

Volunteer to Serve, Spread Some Smiles!

Ichapore Swapnapuron Society believes that unless members of the civil society are involved proactively in the process of development, sustainable change will not happen. We encourage and invite individuals for volunteer opportunities and volunteer registration, to be an active part of our organization and share the same vision and purpose as us – to work for the welfare of children and their families.

Volunteers are the backbone of every organization – they not only carry the organization’s ideals within them, but also spread the message far and beyond, sensitizing the society towards the cause.
