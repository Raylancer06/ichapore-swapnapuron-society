JANMATARU

A Tree for Every Newborn

JANMATARU is our flagship green initiative that celebrates every birth by planting a tree. It creates a lifelong bond between the child and nature, nurturing responsibility, care, and a greener tomorrow.

Tree of Life: Nurturing Nature from Day One

JANMATARU (meaning "Tree of Life") is a unique grassroots campaign by Ichapore Swapnapuron Society to promote environmental responsibility right from the moment of birth.

For every child born in our partner hospitals and rural communities, we gift a hardy, native sapling to the parents along with a personalized birth certificate and a unique Tree ID. Families are encouraged to plant the sapling in their courtyard or community land, and nurture it as their own child grows.

This initiative not only increases green cover across North 24 Parganas, but also instills an unbreakable sense of affection and responsibility towards Mother Earth in future generations.
