PROJECT UDAAN

Your Help Is Crucial in Eliminating Thalassemia

Empowering adolescents and young adults with carrier screening, scientific counseling, and community awareness to stop the transmission of thalassemia and protect future generations.

Stopping a Preventable Genetic Disorder Before It Starts

The Ichapore Swapnapuron Society is proud to introduce our initiative, Udaan, aimed at raising awareness about thalassemia, a genetic blood disorder that affects millions worldwide.

Through this project, we are dedicated to educating both boys and girls in various schools about the importance of thalassemia carrier testing and how it can help in identifying and managing the disease. Our commitment begins with informing young individuals about the condition and encouraging them to participate in carrier testing so that they can make informed decisions regarding their health and future families.

By hosting workshops, seminars, and interactive sessions in schools, we spread knowledge about causes, symptoms, and preventive measures. Fostering a well-informed society allows us to mitigate risks and inspire proactive health choices.

Carrier Screening & Guidance

Conducting certified blood testing camps in secondary schools and colleges with medical specialists and post-test counseling.

How Udaan Works

A Comprehensive Community Action Plan

Eradicating thalassemia through knowledge, testing, and patient support.

School & College Seminars

Doctors and educators conduct interactive sessions dispelling myths, explaining genetic inheritance, and stressing pre-marital testing.

Affordable Carrier Screening

We organize subsidized or free HPLC blood testing camps so students and young adults know their carrier status before marriage.

Transfusion & Patient Support

Providing blood donation coordination, iron chelation medication aid, and emotional guidance to children already living with thalassemia major.
