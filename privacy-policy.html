Privacy Policy & Terms

Ichapore Swapnapuron Society is committed to protecting your privacy, safeguarding personal donor information, and maintaining ethical standards of non-profit governance.

1. Information Gathering

Ichapore Swapnapuron Society collects information from visitors and donors only when voluntarily submitted—such as making an online or offline donation, filling out our volunteer registration form, or contacting us. This information may include your full name, email address, mailing address, contact phone number, and PAN number (for 80G tax exemption certificates). Browsing on our website remains completely anonymous.

2. Use of Personal Information

Personal information submitted to the society is treated as strictly confidential. We use it solely for official society communications, issuing 80G tax exemption receipts, sending program impact updates, and coordinating field volunteer operations. Under no circumstances do we sell, rent, lease, or trade personal data to commercial third parties. You may request deletion or modification of your contact records at any time by writing to ichaporeswapnapuronsociety@gmail.com.

3. Privacy of E-Mail Lists

Ichapore Swapnapuron Society maintains internal mailing lists for volunteer mobilization and donor updates. Individuals who sign up will only receive official communications from our registered coordinators. We never disclose email subscriber lists to external organizations.

4. Payment Gateway Security

All financial transactions and online contributions are processed through verified, industry-compliant payment gateways using standard 256-bit SSL encryption. We do not store sensitive credit/debit card numbers or net banking passwords on our local servers.

5. Refund and Cancellation Policy

As a charitable non-profit organization, all voluntary contributions, online donations, and sponsorships are immediately committed towards purchasing student books, pediatric blood vials, hospital medicines, reflective dog collars, or winter blankets. Consequently, donations once processed are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled. If an accidental duplicate transaction occurs due to technical gateway error, please notify us within 48 hours at ichaporeswapnapuronsociety@gmail.com with payment proof.

6. Terms and Conditions of Website Usage

By accessing and browsing this website, you agree to comply with all applicable laws and use the content exclusively for lawful and non-commercial purposes. Content, reports, and photographs depicting our children, volunteers, and beneficiaries remain the intellectual property of Ichapore Swapnapuron Society and may not be reproduced commercially without prior written authorization.

7. Contact & Governance Queries

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy policies or statutory compliance, please contact our administrative office:
