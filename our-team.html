Our Team

Behind every initiative and every milestone is a dedicated team of individuals working together with passion, empathy, and commitment to create a better tomorrow for our community.

supervised_user_circle Leadership & Governance

Our Board of Management

Guiding our mission with vision, integrity, and lifelong dedication to community welfare.

person

Mr. Sandip Pramanik

Founder & Strategic Lead

Mr. Sandip Pramanik, a graphic designer and alumnus of Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan Salt Lake, is an expert in strategic management and organization building. In 2009, Mr. Pramanik joined hands with like-minded people to found the Ichapore Swapnapuron Society. Since its inception, he has led the scaling of the organization, helping it transform from a small veranda initiative into a well-known social enterprise serving thousands of lives across Bengal.

person_4

Mrs. Beauty Mondal

Philanthropist & Social Worker

A social worker and devoted housewife, Mrs. Beauty Mondal is an expert in entrepreneurship development. As an investor and philanthropist, she brings multifaceted experience in various fields across Hooghly. She has been deeply involved in grassroots social change through education, healthcare, and humanitarian initiatives for more than a decade.

person

Mr. Bejon Mondal

Founding Member & Advisor

Mr. Bijan Mandal is a retired government employee who skillfully managed school administrative operations through dedicated practice for over 35 years. Beyond his career, Mr. Mandal is driven by a deep sense of social duty, quietly contributing to education and community causes. His long-standing commitment to giving back has supported the society since its very inception.

person_4

Mrs. Tapati Mondal

Founding Member

A devoted housewife for whom social service is a true calling. A steadfast champion of community welfare and child protection, Mrs. Tapati Mondal has been a founding pillar of Ichapore Swapnapuron Society since 2009, constantly advocating for the nutritional and emotional support of street children.

person_4

Mrs. Dipali Pramanick

Elder Member & Mentor

An esteemed elder member who embodies the spirit of dedication and service. Seamlessly balancing family responsibilities with impactful social work, she has been a steadfast pillar of wisdom and emotional support since 2009. Her compassionate nature and unwavering commitment continue to enrich the lives of everyone around her.

Grassroots Backbone

Our Volunteers & Field Champions

Beyond the Board of Management, the real pulse of Swapnapuron Society beats through our passionate volunteer corps. From doctors who volunteer weekend clinical hours at our mobile camps to college youth teaching English phonics and animal lovers tagging street dogs with safety collars, they make real change possible every single day.
