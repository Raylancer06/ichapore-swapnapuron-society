Ayushman Bhava

Reflective Collar Initiative for Street Dogs

Ayushman Bhava is our grassroots animal welfare campaign protecting street dogs from fatal night road accidents by fitting durable, high-visibility reflective collars.

Protecting Our Voiceless Friends in the Dark

Every night on busy highways, markets, and dimly lit suburban roads, countless street dogs suffer fatal collisions simply because speeding drivers cannot see them until it is too late.

Ayushman Bhava aims to make them visible, safe, and respected by outfitting street dogs with high-grade, neon reflective collars. When vehicle headlights shine even hundreds of meters away, the glowing collar immediately warns drivers to slow down.

Beyond physical collars, our volunteer drives educate neighborhood residents, drivers, and shopkeepers to treat community animals with kindness and responsibility.
